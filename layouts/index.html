Hamse Abdi Software Engineer, Agentic AI, Atlanta

I build AI agents, the evals that gate them, and the platform they run on.

At Travelers I took a generative AI pilot to production as the underwriters' daily workbench, which cut case review from roughly 6 hours to 30 minutes. Then I designed and built the multi-agent renewal system that is now in pilot with early users.

On my own time I build agents in the open: Argus, a multi-agent code reviewer that reviews its own pull requests, and Stride, an SMS coach that texts first.

What I build at Travelers

About me
Applied AI
The underwriting workbench, in daily use. A nightly job summarizes each policy's claim notes with citations and answers the standard review questions ahead of time.
Agents
A multi-agent renewal system on the Strands SDK and Bedrock AgentCore: 3 supervisors and 11 evaluators, each scoring a policy on an underwriter rubric. The guardrails are code, not prompts. Agents never write to policy systems, and an underwriter approves every adverse decision.
Platform
The eval gate every prompt and model change has to pass, on a golden dataset labeled by underwriter SMEs. Under it, a REST API platform that 3 product teams adopted, an MCP gateway with Okta for identity, and OpenTelemetry tracing.
